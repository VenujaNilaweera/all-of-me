# Career & Interview Preparation

## Preferred answer format
Definition → why it matters → simple example → possible interview
question. Answers should be short (2-4 lines), accurate, and easy to
memorize — not long theoretical explanations.

## Topics covered
- **C:** arrays, functions, pointers, structures, loops, memory,
  pass-by-value/reference, bitwise ops, embedded C.
- **Microcontrollers:** PIC, ARM, STM32, Raspberry Pi, Arduino, ESP32.
- **Embedded systems:** GPIO, UART, PWM, ADC, timers, interrupts,
  communication.
- **PCB:** stackup, differential pairs, impedance, routing, ground
  planes, trace geometry, component placement.
- **VLSI:** basic concepts (floorplanning, clock gating, timing paths —
  see [[vlsi-physical-design]]).
