# Engineering Laboratory Experience

Labs completed as part of the University of Jaffna engineering
coursework.

## Electronics / electrical
Oscilloscope, analog ohmmeter, transformer, RC circuits, RL circuits, GPS
lab.

## Mechanical / materials
Tensile strength, compressive strength, impact test, bending of beams,
Young's modulus, truss.

## Thermal / fluid
Thermal conductivity, manometer, IC engine, Marecet boiler bench-top
unit, Boys gas calorimeter.

## Civil / material testing
- Concrete cube compression (M15, 7-day curing): 20.15, 23.41, 21.084.
- Young's modulus (aluminium), experimental value ~7.06 x 10^10 Pa
  (~70 GPa) — in line with theoretical expectation for aluminium.
