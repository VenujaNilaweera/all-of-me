# Mentor & Faculty Message Templates

Reusable templates and conventions for messaging academic mentors and
lecturers.

## Address conventions
- Address mentors and faculty as **"Sir"**, **"Madam"**, or
  **"Lecturer"**.

## Template categories in regular use
- Project update messages.
- Meeting request messages.
- Internship inquiry messages (focus on opportunities near **Jaffna** and
  **Colombo**).
