# Looting Detection Using Satellite Images

Research project: detect potential looting/damage at archaeological sites
from satellite imagery.

## Dataset
DAFA LS dataset — labeled, spans 2016-2023, 64,000+ images.

## Research gaps identified
- Dependence on long temporal sequences and their computational cost.
- Missing-frame robustness — how well the system performs when temporal
  data is incomplete.

## Evaluation metrics considered
Accuracy, precision, ROC-AUC, confusion matrix.

Demonstrates applying AI to a real-world scientific/archaeological
problem rather than only benchmark datasets.
