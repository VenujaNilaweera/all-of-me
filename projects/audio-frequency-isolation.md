# Audio Frequency Isolation

MATLAB signal-processing application to pull specific sounds out of
background noise.

## Scope
Isolate safety-critical audio cues (fire alarms, vehicle horns) while
rejecting ambient traffic noise.

## Technical details
- Bandpass filtering.
- Smooth gain boosting on the target band.
- Target isolated frequencies: fire alarms and vehicle horns.
- Target rejected noise: background traffic noise.
