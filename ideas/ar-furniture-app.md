# AR Furniture App & 3D Scanning

## Idea
An augmented-reality app that places accurately-scanned furniture models
into the user's real room.

## Open questions / research
- How do handheld 3D scanners actually work (structured light, photogrammetry,
  LiDAR)?
- What accuracy is needed for furniture placement to feel correct?
- Which mobile AR stack to target (ARKit, ARCore, WebXR)?
