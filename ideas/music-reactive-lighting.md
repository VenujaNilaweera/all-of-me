# Music-Reactive Lighting

## Idea
An edge-AI project extracting musical characteristics — beat,
tempo/speed, drops, rises/build-ups — from audio in real time (not just
genre classification) and using them to drive hardware lighting effects.

## Direction
Music → AI/signal processing → ESP32/Raspberry Pi → hardware lighting.

## Related background
Builds on existing interest in audio/music synthesis, Fourier series,
signal processing, and beat/tempo detection. Combines a personal/creative
interest (orchestral percussion, VST production — see
[[music-vst-interests]]) with embedded + AI engineering.
