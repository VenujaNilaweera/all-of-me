# Beta-Wave Focus Enhancer

## Idea
A signal-processing tool that generates or shapes audio around beta-wave
frequencies to help the listener concentrate.

## Open questions
- Which beta-band range gives the best subjective focus?
- Pure tones, binaural beats, or embedded in ambient audio?
- How to measure whether it actually works?
